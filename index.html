About the App

Oil & Gas Calculator provides commonly used formulas and reference calculations for surface well testing and production engineering. The app helps engineers and students quickly estimate flow rates, perform choke and orifice calculations, and access common unit conversions.

All results are estimates and intended for engineering reference and educational use only.

Features

  • Gas and oil flow-rate estimation
  • Choke-based and orifice-based calculations
  • Unit conversions for daily field operations
  • Clean and fast interface for quick calculations
